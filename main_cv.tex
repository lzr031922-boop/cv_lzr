\begin{rSection}{RESEARCH Experience}

\begin{rSubsection}{Equilibrium Reconstruction Algorithm and Magnetic Control of Plasma Shape on the Spherical Tokamak SUNIST-2}{Jul. 2024 - Present}{Department of Plasma Control, Startorus Fusion}{}

\item Developed the equilibrium reconstruction algorithm for doublet / droplet plasma configurations
\item Developed the first real-time equilibrium reconstruction algorithm over a $33 \times 65$ grid with 118 magnetic diagnostics, achieving $\sim$200 $\mu$s per reconstruction cycle
\item Realized preliminary magnetic control of plasma shape based on the reconstructed equilibrium
\item Contributed to device design and led control-algorithm development within the plasma control team, coordinating the workflow from modeling to real-time implementation

\end{rSubsection}
